# Layout provenance and limits

Reference source: Ed Collins, "Ed's Dig Dug Page", last updated June 7, 2012:
https://edcollins.com/digdug/

Publicly viewable arcade screenshots inspected for rounds 1-6:

- Grid reference: https://edcollins.com/digdug/digdug-grid.gif

We measured tunnel segments, rock cells and visible creature positions from these screenshots, then expanded the 14-column reference grid into our 32x19 game grid. These are source-grounded adaptations, not pixel-exact arcade maps. The creatures in a screenshot may already have moved: visible positions are not a verified original spawn table. No ROMs, original source code or sprite assets were used. The screenshots are research references only and are not bundled with the app.

Round 1: central shaft with a horizontal pocket, upper-left vertical tunnel, upper-right horizontal tunnel, lower-left horizontal green-dragon tunnel, lower-right vertical tunnel. Three red goggle creatures, one green dragon, three rocks. This now replaces the old procedural arrangement.

Round 2: three red, two green, four rocks.
Round 3: three red, two green, four rocks.
Round 4: three red, two green, five rocks.
Round 5: three red, three green, five rocks.
Round 6: one red, four green, four rocks.

Round 7 onward: NOT verified. Repeats our six adapted layouts, with the existing increasing enemy speed. In-game label explicitly says "Adapted repeat N". It does not claim to reproduce the arcade's later progression.

Disagreement: the modern guide https://www.speedrun.com/dig_dug_namco/guides/h7b26 describes two rocks in round 1. The arcade screenshot shows three, so this build follows the screenshot. The modern guide points to an Xbox walkthrough, and is not treated as the authority for arcade placement.

Flower row: one symbol per cleared stage, initially zero, with a numeric total and +N overflow when all symbols won't fit. This is the requested cleared-stage counter, not a claim to reproduce every original arcade flower denomination or initial-round display convention.

Still simplified: no dragon fire, bonus fruit, arcade scoring table, exact enemy timing, last-enemy escape or ROM emulation. New original art/code remains under MIT; names and original arcade assets belong to their respective owners. Burrowburst is not official or endorsed.
